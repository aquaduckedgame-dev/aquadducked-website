Delete Your Account or Data

Last updated: June 4, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your AquaDucked! account and all associated data — or just specific portions of your data — at any time. There is no cost, no penalty, and no need to give a reason.

Deleting Specific Data Without Closing Your Account

Several types of data can be removed from inside the Game without contacting us:

  • Published levels: open The Pond, find any level you've published, and use its delete option to remove it permanently.
  • Likes: tap the heart on any level you've liked to unlike it.
  • Follows: visit another user's profile and tap Unfollow.
  • Collections: any collection you own can be unpublished or deleted from the collection's menu.

If you'd like to delete a category of data that the Game doesn't expose a button for — for example, "clear all my cloud progress but keep my account," or "delete every ghost replay I've uploaded" — email aquaduckedgame@gmail.com with the subject line:

Data Deletion Request

Send the email from the address registered to your account and describe in one sentence what you'd like removed. We'll confirm the request and process it within 7 business days.

Deleting Your Entire Account

To permanently remove your account and everything associated with it, email aquaduckedgame@gmail.com from the email address registered to the account, with the subject line:

Account Deletion Request

You don't need to include any other information — the sending email address is enough to identify the account. We'll process the request within 7 business days and reply to confirm once it's complete.

If you no longer have access to the email address registered to the account, write from any email and include your in-game username in the body. We may ask one or two follow-up questions to verify ownership before proceeding.

What Gets Deleted in a Full Account Deletion

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account record (email address and password hash)
  • Your in-game username and profile
  • All custom levels you have published to The Pond
  • Your follows, likes, and any social connections
  • Your cloud-synced level progress, stars, and best times
  • Any ghost replays you have uploaded
  • Any moderation reports you have filed

What Is Retained

For legal and operational reasons, we may retain the following in a non-identifying form:

  • Aggregate analytics — counts of total plays, downloads, and similar statistics that do not identify you individually.
  • Moderation records — if your account was the subject of moderation action (e.g. a level reported and removed), an anonymized record may be kept to enforce community guidelines.
  • Backups — encrypted database backups may briefly retain your data for up to 30 days as part of standard disaster-recovery rotation, after which they are overwritten.

Local Game Data

Deleting your online account does not affect any progress or settings saved locally on your device. To remove local game data, uninstall the app or clear its data from your device's settings.
